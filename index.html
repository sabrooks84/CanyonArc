About Canyon Arc

Subject-matter expertise is only useful if it can be taught, practiced, and evaluated.

Canyon Arc was created around a recurring training problem: organizations often possess deep operational expertise but lack the time or instructional-design capacity to turn that knowledge into a coherent learning system.

Our work focuses on that translation — from doctrine, policy, standards, and field experience into training that instructors can deliver consistently and learners can apply under real-world conditions.

Founder & Principal

Steven A. Brooks

Steven is a public-safety instructor and curriculum developer with more than 15 years of law-enforcement experience and extensive academy instructional experience. His work has included curriculum redevelopment, scenario-based instruction, practical skills, use-of-force and legal instruction, assessment design, instructor development, and training-program implementation.

Canyon Arc was built from the perspective of the instructor responsible for making policy, legal standards, and subject-matter expertise work in the classroom, on the training floor, and under evaluation.
